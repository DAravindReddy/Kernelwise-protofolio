// Engineering Discipline

The 5-Stage SDLC Delivery Framework

How we take your hardware from unboxing to automated production verification in 2 to 4 weeks.

01

Architecture & HW Audit

Pin multiplexing review, power sequencing analysis, and peripheral bus mapping before code execution.

02

Virtual Bring-Up

DTS validation, synthetic sensor emulation, and kernel symbol verification to catch driver flaws early.

03

Physical Bench Test

Digital oscilloscope probe of I2C/SPI lines, interrupt latency verification, and thermal throttling audits.

04

CI/CD & QEMU Gating

Automated pipeline containerization preventing regressions from ever reaching physical dev boards.

05

Handoff & Artifacts

Cryptographic build manifests, clean documentation, and full IP transfer to your engineering team.
